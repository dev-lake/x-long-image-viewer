A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS.

A few things to know.

Which browsers and websites are supported?+

Chrome and Microsoft Edge 116 or later are supported. Once installed, the viewer works with media images on x.com and twitter.com. It does not take over images on other websites.

Do I need to create an account or sign in?+

The extension needs no separate account or sign-in. After installing, refresh any open X / Twitter tabs and click a media image. Whether X itself requires you to sign in depends on X’s own access rules.

Does text recognition need internet access or image uploads?+

The OCR engine and language models are bundled with the extension. Recognition runs on your device without an online OCR service. Your browser still needs to fetch the selected image from X’s image server. Recognition results stay temporarily in page memory and are cleared when you refresh.

Will all image text be recognized accurately?+

Recognition currently supports English and Simplified Chinese. Clear printed text works best. Small, rotated, handwritten, or complex layouts may produce errors. Images over 40 million pixels are not processed. Check copied text against the original.

What if the extension does not work after installation?+

Check that the extension is enabled, then refresh your open X / Twitter tabs. Refresh after updating or reloading the extension, too. If the issue continues, report it on GitHub.

Does Liquid Glass work on different devices?+

Image refraction runs on your device. When the refraction effect is unavailable, the toolbar uses frosted glass instead. You can keep viewing images and selecting text.
